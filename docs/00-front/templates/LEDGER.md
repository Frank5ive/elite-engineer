# Competency Ledger

The ledger records what you can **demonstrate**, not what you have read. Update it at the end of every lesson (section 12 of each lesson) and review it every week (WEEKLY-REVIEW.md).

## Status definitions

| Status | Meaning | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Unseen | Not started | none |
| Introduced | Read or watched; exercises not (yet) passed. Reading alone is never more than this. | Date read |
| Practiced | Exercises attempted in Mode A or B, with mistakes understood | Link to attempt and failure log entries |
| Demonstrated | Mastery gate passed (80/100, no critical failure); you can reproduce and explain without the lesson | Link to graded project and gate score |
| Retained | Re-tested after at least 14 days (cold, no notes) and passed | Link to the retest and its date |
| Applied | Used correctly in real work or in a later project, without prompting | Link to the commit, PR, or project |
| Production-ready | Used in a deployed system, operated or reviewed by another person, survived a real defect or change | Link to deployment/review evidence |

Rules: a status may go down if a retest fails. Do not jump statuses without the evidence of the intermediate one. Keep evidence links as repository-relative paths or URLs. Dates are ISO (YYYY-MM-DD).
